## Our Mission

BIT-CENTRAL streamlines student life at BIT Sathy by providing instant access to academic materials, daily mess menus, exam hall venues, reward point tracking, and important campus resources — all in one secure, user-friendly platform. Thousands of students across all departments and batches at Bannari Amman Institute of Technology use BIT-CENTRAL every day and find it genuinely helpful for their academic journey.

---

## Platform Features

- **Academic Resources** — Access semester-wise question banks, answer keys, and study materials organised by department and batch.
- **Mess Menu** — Real-time hostel mess menu with daily updates for both hostels at BIT Sathy.
- **RP Site Integration** — Direct access to the Reward Points (RP) system for tracking co-curricular and extracurricular achievements.
- **Reward Points → Internal Marks** — View your earned Reward Points which are officially converted into internal marks, helping you keep track of how your activities contribute to your academic score.
- **Exam Hall Venues** — Check your semester exam hall and venue details directly on the platform — no more last-minute confusion about which block or room you're assigned to.
- **Smart Profile** — Automatic department and batch detection from your college email, so you only see what's relevant to you.

---

## Reward Points & Internal Marks

BIT Sathy's Reward Points (RP) system rewards students for participation in technical events, sports, cultural activities, NSS, NCC, and other campus achievements. These earned Reward Points are **officially converted into internal marks** as part of your academic evaluation.

BIT-CENTRAL gives you a clear, real-time view of:

- Total Reward Points earned so far
- Breakdown by activity or event category
- How your RP score translates into your internal mark contribution
- Direct link to the official RP portal for verification

This helps students plan their participation strategically and never miss out on marks they've already earned.

## Exam Hall Venues

Searching notice boards or waiting for WhatsApp forwards to find your exam hall is a thing of the past. BIT-CENTRAL displays your **semester exam hall venue** directly on the platform, including:

- Your assigned hall or room number
- Block and floor details
- Exam schedule aligned with your department and batch

Students across all departments at Bannari Amman Institute of Technology rely on this feature every semester to locate their exam venue quickly and confidently.
