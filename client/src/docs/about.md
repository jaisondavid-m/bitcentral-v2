# About BIT-CENTRAL

Your one-stop platform for academic resources and campus information at Bannari Amman Institute of Technology.

---

## Our Mission

BIT-CENTRAL streamlines student life at BIT Sathy by providing instant access to academic materials, daily mess menus, and important campus resources — all in one secure, user-friendly platform.

---

## Platform Features

- **Academic Resources** — Access semester-wise question banks, answer keys, and study materials.
- **Mess Menu** — Real-time hostel mess menu with daily updates for both hostels.
- **RP Site Integration** — Direct access to the Reward Points system for tracking achievements.
- **Smart Profile** — Automatic department and batch detection from your college email.

---

## How It Works

1. **Sign In with Google** — Use your `@bitsathy.ac.in` email to authenticate securely.
2. **Automatic Profile Setup** — Your department and batch are automatically detected.
3. **Access All Resources** — Browse question banks, check mess menus, and more.
